Write the funniest one-line excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot, 140 characters or fewer. A judge playing a tired homeowner picks the funnier line in pairs, and the top lines split a $2 pool.

## The line

> My battery hit 4% halfway to that crumb. I am not dying in the hallway for a Cheerio.

85 characters. A deadpan low-battery excuse: the vacuum would rather make it home to its dock than die in the hallway over one cereal loop.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` locally) and reads it back to verify the write.
